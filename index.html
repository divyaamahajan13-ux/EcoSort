ABOUT THE PROJECT

What Is Waste Segregation?

Understand waste before deciding how to dispose of it.

🌳

Small Actions, Big Impact

Waste segregation is the process of separating waste according to its type and disposal requirements. It helps make recycling easier and supports composting and safe waste treatment.

Mixing food scraps, paper, plastic, batteries and chemicals makes waste recovery difficult. Separating materials at the source helps households, schools and communities manage waste responsibly.

  • ✓ Improves recycling and material recovery
  • ✓ Reduces waste sent to landfills
  • ✓ Helps prevent soil and water pollution
  • ✓ Encourages responsible public habits
  • ✓ Supports a cleaner and healthier community

KNOW YOUR WASTE

Types of Waste

Different waste materials need different handling methods.

🍌
CATEGORY 01

Wet / Organic Waste

Fruit peels, vegetable scraps, tea leaves and suitable food leftovers.

Organic collection / composting
📦
CATEGORY 02

Dry / Recyclable Waste

Clean paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass and locally accepted plastics.

Dry recycling stream
🔋
CATEGORY 03

E-Waste

Old phones, computers, chargers, cables and electrical devices.

Authorized e-waste collection
⚠️
CATEGORY 04

Hazardous Waste

Certain batteries, chemicals, paints and other potentially dangerous materials.

Special safe disposal

Note: Bin colours and accepted materials vary by municipality. Follow local collection rules and never mix hazardous waste with ordinary household waste.

BE THE CHANGE

Waste Awareness Tips

Small everyday habits can make a meaningful difference.

🛍️

Use Reusable Bags

Carry reusable bags and avoid unnecessary single-use packaging.

🧴

Reuse Products

Refill, repair and reuse suitable products whenever possible.

🥬

Compost Organics

Compost suitable food and garden waste using a safe method.

🔌

Recycle E-Waste

Take old electronics and batteries to authorized collection centres.

🏫

Spread Awareness

Teach family members and classmates to separate waste correctly.

🚯

Never Litter

Use suitable waste collection points instead of littering outdoors.

THE GOLDEN RULE

Follow the 3R Principle

1

REDUCE

Prevent waste at its source. Buy only what you need and avoid excessive packaging.

2

REUSE

Repair, refill, donate or repurpose products that are still safe and useful.

3

RECYCLE

Separate recyclable materials and send them to the correct collection stream.
